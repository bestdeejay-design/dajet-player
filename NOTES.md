Dajet Player 0.2.0: knows what it plays. The project builds with SwiftPM, and `./build.sh` wraps the binary into an ordinary Mac app — `build/DajetPlayer.app`, `ru.dajet.player`, ad-hoc signed. Drop a folder of music — or a few files — on the window and it plays them one after another (mp3, m4a, flac, wav, aiff), its title, artist and album read from the file, its cover blurred behind the window. Next and Previous walk the pass (Previous restarting a track once its three seconds have gone, otherwise stepping back; on the first track it restarts), shuffle draws a fresh pass that never repeats a track twice, repeat cycles through off, all and one, and Space stops and starts. Nothing in it can reach the network: no telemetry, no accounts, no cloud, no cover art fetched from somewhere. The keyboard's media keys, progress, volume and the two skins are waves of their own.
