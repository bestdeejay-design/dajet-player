Dajet Player 0.4.0: lives in the tray, and the window knows its minimum. The project builds with SwiftPM, and `./build.sh` wraps the binary into an ordinary Mac app — `build/DajetPlayer.app`, `ru.dajet.player`, ad-hoc signed. Drop a folder of music — or a few files — on the window and it plays them one after another (mp3, m4a, flac, wav, aiff), its title, artist and album read from the file, its cover drawn sharp in the middle of the window: from the file's own tags when it has them, from a picture beside it when it does not. Next and Previous walk the pass (Previous restarting a track once its three seconds have gone, otherwise stepping back; on the first track it restarts), shuffle draws a fresh pass that never repeats a track twice, repeat cycles through off, all and one, and Space stops and starts. The keyboard's media keys are the app's — play, pause, play/pause, next and previous, each a hand on a button that was already there; the Now Playing card in Control Center and on the lock screen carries title, artist, duration and progress and goes away when nothing plays. A thin bar along the foot of the window shows where the track is and takes it where the hand lets go, once dragged; `←` and `→` walk the queue beside Space; volume and mute sit in the bar and are remembered between runs. The window wears the `dajet` look — glass over the whole light window (clearer than it was), matte near-black in the dark — with a side menu of playlists that stays between runs, and the folder's tracks in that menu rather than in the middle of the window. The toolbar above is the system's own: the menu's toggle beside the traffic lights, Open Folder… and the theme at the other end. The player now has an icon in the menu bar as well, with a full menu under it — the current track, the transport, the playlists each with their own submenu, Open Folder…, the theme, settings and Quit — and the icon moves while music plays; the music outlives the window. The window will not shrink below a formal square of 232 points, and when it is that narrow the menu comes out over the player. Nothing in it can reach the network: no telemetry, no accounts, no cloud, no cover art fetched from somewhere. The `joer` skin and the release are waves of their own.
