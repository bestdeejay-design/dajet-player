Dajet Player 0.1.0: the skeleton. The project builds with SwiftPM, and `./build.sh` wraps the binary into an ordinary Mac app — `build/DajetPlayer.app`, `ru.dajet.player`, ad-hoc signed — that opens on one empty window: no toolbar, no sidebar, no title bar. Nothing plays yet, and nothing in it can reach the network: opening a file, metadata, cover art, the keyboard's media keys and the two skins are waves of their own.
