Dajet Player 0.1.0: plays. The project builds with SwiftPM, and `./build.sh` wraps the binary into an ordinary Mac app — `build/DajetPlayer.app`, `ru.dajet.player`, ad-hoc signed. Drop a folder of music — or a few files — on the window and it plays them one after another (mp3, m4a, flac, wav, aiff), Space stopping it and starting it again. Nothing in it can reach the network: no telemetry, no accounts, no cloud, no cover art fetched from somewhere. Metadata, cover art, the keyboard's media keys and the two skins are waves of their own.
